## Shared Image World

The visual world of MetricStack is defined by a "High-Contrast Cosmic-Tech" aesthetic. All imagery must feel surgically clean, sophisticated, and immersive, set against a backdrop of absolute pure black (`#000000`). 

**Core Visual Elements:**
- **Material Language:** Surfaces are either matte dark-grey (`#111111`) or glass-like with 1px crisp, semi-transparent white borders. No drop shadows are permitted; depth is created through color layering and luminous glows.
- **Lighting:** Use a "Darkroom Studio" logic. Light sources are rare and intentional, primarily manifesting as a vibrant neon yellow-green (`#CCFF00`) glow or "rim lighting" on 3D objects.
- **Backgrounds:** Hero sections feature massive, high-fidelity 3D renders of dark planetary bodies, particle clouds, or starfields. These assets should feel vast and cinematic, never cluttered.
- **Product UI:** Dashboard previews must be ultra-crisp, featuring simplified "abstracted" data visualizations (line charts, bar graphs) using neon accents against dark backgrounds.

## Variation Policy

Variation is strictly controlled by the page context to maintain a cohesive narrative journey:
- **Hero Imagery:** Varies by route—Planets for the Homepage, Particle Plexus for the About page, and Starfields for the Blog.
- **Iconography:** Must always be lightweight wireframes with neon green strokes.
- **Photography:** Portraits and team photos must be desaturated or cool-toned to prevent warm skin tones from breaking the dark, high-tech palette.

## Avoid

- **No Drop Shadows:** Do not use soft shadows to create depth; use 1px borders only.
- **No Warmth:** Avoid warm oranges, reds, or "sunny" lighting. The world is cold, dark, and neon.
- **No Clutter:** Avoid busy backgrounds or "stock" office photography.
- **No Solid Icons:** Avoid heavy, filled-in glyphs; stick to thin-stroke wireframes.
- **No Generic UI:** Avoid default browser-looking buttons or standard blue-tinted SaaS dashboards.
